BEHIND THE GARAGE

More than toys.
A passion for cars.

HW Garage is an online die-cast car shop designed for collectors and automotive enthusiasts. We offer a collection of miniature vehicles inspired by iconic sports cars, classic models, and powerful trucks.

Our goal is to make collecting exciting, convenient, and accessible for everyone, from beginners to passionate collectors. Through our user-friendly website, customers can explore different models, discover their favorite cars, and enjoy a smooth shopping experience.

At HW Garage, we believe that small cars bring big thrills, and every model deserves a place in your collection.
